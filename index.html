About

I don't stop at the handoff.
I understand how it gets built.

Based in Lagos, Nigeria, I'm a product designer who thinks in systems. I design interfaces for SaaS, marketplace, and consumer products, grounding every UI decision in user flow, information hierarchy, and business goals, not just visual polish.

What sets me apart: I don't hand off and disappear. I understand implementation well enough to make design decisions that are actually buildable, and I can take a design further than Figma when a project calls for it, through prototyping, AI-assisted development, and shipped code.

I use AI tools like Claude Code and Cursor as part of my design process, for rapid prototyping, design-to-code, and iteration. Not as a shortcut around the thinking, but as a way to test ideas faster.

Process

How I Design

01

Understand the Problem

I start with the user and the business problem, not the screen. What are people trying to do? What's blocking them? Strategy first, UI after.

02

Structure the Experience

Information architecture and user flow before UI. What's the journey, what's the hierarchy, what can wait until later.

03

Design the Interface

High-fidelity UI, design systems, and prototyping in Figma, with AI tools running alongside for exploring variations quickly.

04

Build & Ship, When It Calls For It

For select projects, I take the design into code myself, using AI-assisted development to move from prototype to working product fast.
