About Me

I design products.
Then I ship them.

Product Designer based in Lagos, Nigeria. I design UI/UX for SaaS, marketplace, and consumer products, and take select designs further than Figma using modern frontend tools and AI-assisted workflows.

The Story

I started with Figma, wireframes, and prototypes, and I'm still a designer first. Along the way I kept running into the same wall: good design sitting in handoff limbo, waiting on developers with different priorities. So I taught myself to build, not to become an engineer, but so my designs never had to lose their intent in translation.

Today I design UI/UX for SaaS, marketplace, and mobile products, thinking in flows and systems rather than isolated screens. For a subset of projects, where it genuinely helps the client, I also take the design into working code, using React, Next.js, and AI-assisted development to move from prototype to shipped product fast.

Design and implementation aren't separate disciplines to me. They're two lenses on the same problem, and understanding both is what lets me make UI decisions that are ambitious but still buildable.

What I Believe

Good design earns its place by solving a real user or business problem, not just by looking clean. Every screen should be able to answer "why does this exist" before it answers "how does this look."

A designer who understands implementation makes better decisions, because trade-offs stop being abstract. I lean on that understanding constantly, without letting it turn every project into an engineering exercise.

AI tools like Claude Code and Cursor have changed how fast a designer can validate an idea. I use them to prototype and iterate faster, not to skip the thinking that makes a product actually work.
